### Late May

Clusters of baby grapes are everywhere. I'm removing most of them this year, though, so the vines can spend their energy on their trunk and roots.

I'm looking to train the vines into VSP Single Guyot. We'll see if I'm competent enough to pull that off.

### Late June

The vines are growing vigorously. A new mulch layer of hemp, wood chips, and cacao shells was applied.

![Late June](/images/2026/late_june.webp "Late June")

![Late June Mulching](/images/2026/late_june_mulching.webp "Late June Mulching")

### Mid July

Some of the vines, especially those in the right row, seem to be developing a magnesium deficiency.
A foliar spray was applied after taking the below picture.
Last year, there were some differences in the exact ratios of the terraforming mix between the rows.
I wonder if that is the cause of this disbalance. Or perhaps there are local differences in how the lime was applied?
