## Measure of a Mortal

![Measure of a Mortal](/images/paladin_optimized.webp "Measure of a Mortal"){: style="display: block; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;"}

The _Measure of a Mortal_ is the central image of Mythopoetic Stoic Absurdism.

**Antique Gold (#C8A05A)** lends the emblem the _gravitas_ of an ancient sacred object, while **Ivory (#F0E5C9)** gives the cloaked figure a finite, embodied presence against the **Void (#0B0B0A)**. The figure could be a **🗡️Paladin of the 🛡Order of the Open 👁Eye**; the lowered sword may suggest _prohairesis_: the capacity for deliberate, virtuous action without sovereignty over outcomes. Above, the radiant darkness remains unresolved: an eye that may never return the gaze, an eclipse veiling the painful truth of **🔆Sol Invictus**, or a black hole whose luminous boundary conceals what cannot be known. Such ambiguity might evoke the Absurd itself: the encounter between a questioning consciousness and a cosmos that supplies no answer. The fine orbit may be the mortal thread of the Fates, joining **🧵Clotho**'s unchosen beginnings, **📏Lachesis**' unfolding circumstances, and **✂️Atropos**' inevitable ending; its apparent refusal to pass behind the central darkness could place any final revelation beyond the human lifespan. A clockwise reading lets measurement follow the actions of the 🗡️Paladin, rather than prescribing them. The rocky base might recall **🪨Sisyphus**, or standing atop the trials of **🦅Nietzschean** becoming. The interrupted encompassing ring could evoke **🌌Iuppiter Optimus Maximus**, cosmic totality without a promise of moral perfection or final telos, or perhaps simply the incompleteness of any human individual. If the centre is an eclipsed sun, the scattered stars might suggest other lights and other 🗡️Paladins, distant yet capable of mutually recognizing each other: solitude opening toward solidarity in an unfinished, ongoing 📜Quest.
