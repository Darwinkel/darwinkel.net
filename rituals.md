Rituals and practices are based on both classical Stoic exercises, as well as those coming from CBT, REBT, ACT, and mindfulness (see [Apology](/apology)).
The below list is preliminary and experimental, and will be refined in the future.

### 🎭 PRAYERS (3 - Anchor Psychological Axes)

**Prayer to Fortuna**
> O Fortuna, spinning mother of chaos,
> Grant me the calm to embrace what I must,
> The fire to revolt when I can,
> And the wit to know the tragic difference.

**Invocation of Thanatos**
> O Thanatos, silent usher,
> Remind me I am dying,
> And thus must live.

**Mantra of the Rebel**
> I defy meaninglessness,
> By becoming meaning.

### 🕯️ SHRINES (4 - "The Four Altars")

**Shrine of Thanatos**

* **Symbol**: Hourglass or skull
* **Use**: Whisper *"Memento Mori"* at dawn
* **Purpose**: Value alignment, fear inoculation

**Shrine of Sisyphus**

* **Symbol**: Pebble or boulder
* **Use**: Move a stone daily. Let go.
* **Purpose**: ACT-style acceptance, behavioral momentum

**Shrine of the Sage**

* **Symbol**: Scrolls, coins, or bust of Marcus
* **Use**: Declare one virtue aloud each morning
* **Purpose**: Stoic self-construction and reinforcement

**Shrine of Lethe**

* **Symbol**: Flowing water or foggy mirror
* **Use**: Write one rigid belief, then burn or dissolve it
* **Purpose**: Ritual distancing, ACT-style defusion

### 📜 RITUALS

**Morning Rite of Constraint and Action** *(2–3 min)*

1. Whisper a fear aloud. Imagine it vividly.
2. Breathe: Inhale 3, hold 4, exhale 5.
3. Recite:

> "As Ananke binds me, I accept. As Reason frees me, I act."

**Evening Socratic Triad** *(5–10 min journaling)*

* **Stoic Sage**: "What virtue did I neglect?"
* **Camusian Rebel**: "What meaning did I make?"
* **The Dog**: "Where did I lie to myself?"
  *Optional*: End by extinguishing a candle or using Shrine of Lethe.

**Ritual of Sacred Vitality** *(Daily)*

* Perform a bodily act (cold exposure, walk, pushups)
* Whisper:

> "This is how I praise the fact of life."

**Ritual of Imaginal Reclamation** *(Trauma Processing)*

* Recall a shaming memory
* Imagine a third-party protector (e.g. Mercury) intervening
* Rescript the outcome
* Burn symbol of old belief in Shrine of Lethe

**Ritual of Self-Compassion Liturgy**
*Prayer:*

> I speak now not to scold, but to soothe.
> I suffer, as do all mortals.
> May I greet this pain as I would a friend:
> gently, patiently, with truth.

**Ritual of Connection** *(Weekly)*

* Share a suffering and the meaning it produced
* Toast: *"To the absurd journey we share."*
  *Optional*: Feast of Fools (masked dinner with story-sharing)

### 🎉 SACRED HOLIDAYS - The Triumvirate of Holy Madness

**Festival of Sisyphus** *(Summer Solstice)*

* Move stones. Affirm resilience. Eat grapes.
* Symbolizes: Camusian absurdity, persistence, joy in labor.

**Dies Contra Fortunam** *(January 17)*

* Mock Fate. Share misfortunes with laughter.
* REBT-style exposure and reframing.
* Toast: *"To our glorious misfortunes - may they teach better than Fate."*

**Saturnalia** *(December 17–23)*

* Invert roles. Consume absurd meals. Praise wine.
* Catharsis, joyous disorder, irreverence.
* Toast: *"To Fate, the fool! We drink in defiance!"*
